# Stage 5 — major growth surfaces and whole-subject integration

## Image and preserved basis
The original attached 302×404 photograph was inspected as pixels. Its silver weathered trunk and narrow reddish living growth, overlapping asymmetric crown tiers and dark branch windows inform the tree. The selected PC garden image was re-viewed beside the v23 actual WebGL scene and cage-v06 clay. The target image is a visual reference only; it is never a runtime background or a warped substitute for 3D.

The selected v06 cage and its unapplied Catmull–Clark interpolation are the editing basis. Preserve its solid support volume. Work on actual shared cage vertices, keep the root contact outline, and vary branch thickness and curved centres. Do not import the old ribbon trunk, remake an implicit field, cut holes or add a detached living tube. Local major planes may indent a substantial core but must not reduce the trunk to a sheet. The living and dead portions are properties on this one surface. Fine wood grain is deferred.

## Composition and light
The full subject combines a broad irregular left lower crown, lighter rear-left middle layer, apex set slightly right, right crown and subordinate low right growth. Keep openings between these masses. Fine closed scale shoots and visible supporting twigs are reusable botanical components, not sphere surfaces or leaf cards. Their root paths must attach to the new branches. The shallow unglazed pot and low stone retain the established one-metre scale.

PC: wide crown against open blue-hour garden space, warm veranda off the right, complete pot/stone contact. Mobile 390/320: individually fitted camera, shallow three-quarter angle that preserves the front branch windows, enough horizontal field to contain the complete crown and enough vertical presence to include the low stone. Do not solve width only by turning the tree into a side view. Minimum existing night lighting and material differences are sufficient to judge integration; detailed garden/architecture is the next stage only if the hero supports it.
