# Stage 3: two local structure candidates

The source is the preserved v20 coarse solid; v23 remains the protected runtime candidate. No full archive or old model is copied. The new-data budget is 300 MiB and the minimum free disk floor is 2 GiB. At most two structural candidates will be made within two hours.

Candidate A changes the root surface itself: outer roots descend below the existing soil instead of ending in three repeated white claws. It then uses individually shaped closed cutter meshes for three unequal deadwood losses. This changes the actual surface and edge geometry; it does not add periodic grain or rely on darker material. Branches and the existing rear supporting mass remain the basis.

The gate compares neutral clay at front and +/-25 degrees, with the same physical camera envelope. Requirements: a rooted base without parallel claws, a deadwood face with unequal thickness and interrupted edges, and continuous branch shoulders without a bent-strip appearance. Closed geometry is required but does not by itself pass the visual gate. Candidate B is allowed only to address a concrete failure visible in A. After the gate, assess the sculpture in the full scene before adding background detail.

Only small source/operation records, candidate GLBs, compact changed-geometry data and representative screenshots are retained. One dedicated new QA profile is reused; the 55 older profiles are untouched. The best native model is saved once after comparison. Existing user files, protected baselines, git history and external publication remain untouched.
